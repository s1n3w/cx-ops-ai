# Voice of the Customer report (sample run on the 200-response core set)

200 responses. NPS **3** (77 promoters, 52 passives, 71 detractors).

## Summary

**Headline**
Tallyline’s overall Net Promoter Score (NPS) stands at 3, based on 200 total responses. The score has shown volatility over the last six months, ranging from a low of -10 in June 2026 to a high of 9 in October 2026. While the most recent month shows improvement, the historical trend indicates instability, with scores fluctuating between -10 and 5 prior to October. The current distribution includes 77 promoters, 52 passives, and 71 detractors. Scores vary slightly by plan, with Pro users reporting an NPS of 4, while Business and Starter plans both report an NPS of 2.

**What's driving detractors**
Reliability and billing are the primary sources of dissatisfaction. The top detractor theme is "bugs_reliability" with 24 mentions, followed closely by "billing_pricing" (19 mentions) and "support_speed" (18 mentions). Customers are reporting critical issues that impact trust and operations. Specific examples include unauthorized charges for removed seats, late payouts affecting payroll, and data loss of time entries. Additionally, security concerns have been raised regarding client invoice leaks, and users report frustration with slow support responses during outages or billing disputes.

**Recommended actions**
*   Prioritize immediate investigation into billing discrepancies and unauthorized charges, as these are directly linked to churn threats and bank disputes.
*   Address the "bugs_reliability" theme by auditing recent outages and data loss incidents to restore trust in system stability, particularly for Business plan users.
*   Improve support response times for high-severity issues, ensuring clear communication during outages or billing errors to prevent escalation.
